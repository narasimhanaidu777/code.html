HR Policies

Leave Policy

Overview

Employees are entitled to 20 paid leaves per year (pro-rated for new joiners), plus public holidays. Requests should be submitted through HR at least 3 days in advance when possible.

Work from Home (WFH)

Guidelines

Employees may work remotely up to two days a week with manager approval. Maintain availability during core hours and ensure secure handling of company data.

Code of Conduct

Professional expectations

Respect, integrity and professionalism are expected at all times. Harassment or discrimination is not tolerated — report concerns to HR confidentially.

Performance Reviews & Promotions

Cycle & criteria

Annual performance reviews take place every December. Promotions are based on impact, skills, and business needs.
